Volant Reads: Customer and Owner Guide

This note explains the main store workflows, what each user should do, and what owners must verify before production.

Important: This is an operational guide, not a replacement for security rules, payment verification, privacy terms, or legal advice.

Customers and Readers

  • Create an account and keep your email and password secure.
  • Browse approved books from the store and open a book's details page before purchasing.
  • Check the book title, author, price, currency, and available reading information before payment.
  • After a successful purchase, open your dashboard library and select the book to read.
  • Use the reader controls to move between pages, zoom, and scroll through the fetched PDF.
  • On phones, the reader is designed for vertical scrolling and touch gestures. Keep the browser at normal zoom.
  • Use Share to share the book link. Do not share purchased files or private account information.
  • Save a book for offline reading only when you understand that cached access depends on the browser and device.
  • Use the account deletion request form when you want the owner to review deletion of your account and related data.

Buyers and Payments

  • Sign in before checkout so the purchase can be associated with your account.
  • Use the cart or single-book checkout in details.html.
  • Complete payment through Paystack and wait for verification to finish.
  • Do not close or refresh during the payment callback unless the payment provider has finished.
  • Keep the transaction reference and payment receipt until the book appears in your library.
  • Contact the store owner with the book ID, account email, transaction reference, and receipt if access is missing.
  • Do not pay through unofficial links or send payment credentials to an author.

Authors and Book Owners

  • Submit books through submit.html with accurate title, author, category, description, pricing, currency, and download limits.
  • Upload a readable PDF and a suitable cover image. Confirm that you own or are licensed to distribute every uploaded work.
  • Configure Paystack payout information and verify the returned subaccount details before selling.
  • Track books, sales, earnings, downloads, analytics, messages, and wishlist activity from the dashboard.
  • Do not assume that submission means publication. Books normally remain pending until an administrator approves them.
  • Review book metadata and pricing before asking customers to purchase.
  • Respond to customer issues and deletion or rights requests promptly.

Administrators

  • Review pending books in approvals.html or the admin panel before approval.
  • Check PDF quality, cover quality, metadata, ownership claims, pricing, and policy compliance.
  • Approve only legitimate works. Record why a work is rejected or removed.
  • Review account deletion requests from deletion_requests.
  • Confirm the requester identity before processing deletion.
  • Keep an auditable record of approval, rejection, deletion, and support actions.
  • Protect administrator accounts and never expose service credentials or Firebase configuration secrets.

Account Deletion

  • The user submits a request with a reason from the store dashboard or profile page.
  • The request is stored in deletion_requests and the user record is marked as requesting deletion.
  • An administrator reviews, approves, or rejects the request from the admin interface.
  • Processing currently targets user-related poetry, drafts, social records, conversations, notifications, classic poems, admin records, and mailing entries.
  • Before production, confirm whether Firebase Authentication accounts, books, purchases, book reads, Cloudinary files, and the deletion request record must also be removed or retained.
  • Deletion should follow the site's privacy policy and applicable data-protection requirements.

Reading and Offline Behavior

  • The reader checks book ownership before displaying protected book content.
  • The local PDF.js reader renders fetched PDF pages in a vertical scroll area.
  • Desktop uses a centered reader navigation bar. Smaller screens keep their own mobile reader layout.
  • Zoom applies to the PDF pages, while reader controls remain fixed.
  • PDFs and book metadata may be cached locally for offline reading.
  • Readers should clear local data or sign out on shared devices.

Production Checklist

  • Add and verify Firestore security rules. No rules file was found during this review.
  • Move privileged operations to trusted server-side code or Cloud Functions where appropriate.
  • Server-side payment verification must validate the authenticated user, book, author, amount, currency, and transaction reference.
  • Reconcile Paystack webhooks with purchases instead of relying only on client-side writes.
  • Test multi-item carts, non-default currencies, failed payments, retries, duplicate callbacks, and refunds.
  • Restrict Cloudinary upload presets and validate uploaded file types and sizes.
  • Define complete deletion and retention behavior for every data store and uploaded asset.
  • Test the reader on desktop, Android, iPhone, and narrow phone widths, including scrolling, zooming, sharing, and refresh.
  • Remove unused or duplicate payment and reader implementations where possible.
  • Never commit service-account keys or other credentials to the public deployment.
