About

Poet, Author & AI Assistant

Rence Blunt
I write stories with heart and build software with soul. Every line — of poetry or of code — is an attempt to make someone feel something real. — Rence Blunt

Rence Blunt is a poet and author based in Ghana, whose work lives at the crossroads of the literary and the digital. He is the author of Scentless Roses and The Fall Of Twilight.

As a builder, Rence created the Volant ecosystem: a connected family of platforms where poetry is published, stories are read, lyrics are bought and sold, and creators sell their work — all in one handcrafted corner of the web.

He also works as an AI assistant and vibe-coder — designing intelligent systems and shipping real products through a mix of creative direction and AI-assisted engineering.
