Community Standards

Volant Poetry is an open community — poets can freely share their work without editorial approval. To keep the community respectful and inspiring, we ask every member to follow these simple standards:

Simple Standards:
  • All poems must be written by the author themselves.
  • No AI-generated content is allowed.
  • Respect other poets and readers — no spam or plagiarism.
  • Use proper titles and basic formatting.

Read the full submission guidelines before publishing your work.

The Ranking System

Volant Poetry ranks poems and poets based on genuine community engagement — not just popularity. Commenting thoughtfully on others' work is valued even more than receiving likes, which creates a supportive environment where every poet can thrive.

🏆 Poem Ranking

Poems are ranked on engagement from the last 7 days. Old poems can still win if they receive recent activity!

Score = (Views × 1) + (Likes × 3) + (Comments × 4)
Example: 100 views + 20 likes + 5 comments
= (100×1) + (20×3) + (5×4) = 180 points

Rolling 7 Days: only activity from the last 7 days counts. Poems fall off after 7 days of no activity.

👤 Poet Ranking

Poets are ranked weekly (Monday to Sunday). Winners are announced every Monday!

Score = (Poems × 2) + (Likes Received × 1.5) + (Comments Received × 1.5) + (Likes Given × 3) + (Comments Given × 4)
Key insight: Giving feedback (likes/comments) is worth MORE than receiving it!

Weekly Reset: rankings reset every Monday. The previous week's champion is saved in our Hall of Fame.

Weekly Highlights

  • Poem of the Week — a standout poem hand-picked each week for the community to enjoy.
  • Quote of the Week — an inspiring quote selected weekly to spark reflection.
  • Hall of Fame — previous weekly champions are remembered on the platform.
  • Poetry in Motion — a gallery celebrating poetry through visual art and display.
