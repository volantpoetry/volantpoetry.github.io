Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

What is "vibe coding"?
Vibe coding is our approach to building websites and platforms with speed, creativity, and AI assistance. We listen to your vision and bring it to life with clean, custom code that works beautifully.
How long does a project take?
Basic websites take 3-5 days. Business websites take 5-7 days. Custom platforms take 1-3 weeks depending on features. We'll give you a timeline during consultation.
Do you offer ongoing support?
Yes! We offer maintenance plans starting at ₵20/month or $3/month to keep your site secure, updated, and running smoothly.
What platforms do you build on?
We build custom websites and platforms from the ground up using clean HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. We also work with Firebase, Node.js, and other modern technologies.
What is the process like?
We start with a consultation to understand your vision, then we design, build, and launch your site. We involve you at every step to ensure the result matches your vision.
Do you use AI?
Yes! We use AI tools like Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and ChatGPT to build faster and more efficiently. This allows us to deliver quality work at affordable prices.
